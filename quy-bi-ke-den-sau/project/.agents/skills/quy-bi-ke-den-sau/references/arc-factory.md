# Expansion workflow

After Arc 1 is fully approved and summarized: 1. update canon-drift ledger; 2. map Klein parallel events known to author; 3. review every outstanding mystery, evidence and relationship debt; 4. propose three next-arc scenarios with 20–50 chapter length and different fingerprints; 5. select one with consequences and cost; 6. outline 20–50 chapters; 7. lock solution/reveal checkpoints; 8. write in batches of 2–4 with memory and QA updates; 9. commit local; 10. leave optional future-arc hooks without promising off-screen work.
