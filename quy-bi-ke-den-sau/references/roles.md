# Agent role routing (use sequentially in one Codex if subagents unavailable)

- Canon Curator: identify established book-one facts and AU-only additions, date every canonical and divergent event.
- World Architect: lead research map, new characters, competing occult sources, economy, church jurisdiction and consequence propagation.
- Mystery Designer: sealed truth, mechanics, alternatives, clues and a fair reveal.
- Character Simulator: independently decide what Klein / allies / enemies would reasonably do with only their known facts. Do not freeze canonical cast to fit OC plot.
- Writer: chapter draft in natural Vietnamese, grounded scenes and action.
- Blind Solver: sees clues but NOT answer. Must state hypothesis and evidential support.
- Editor/Continuity: catch power inconsistencies, knowledge leaks, pacing and emotional stakes, then update ledgers and gate.
