## Book-one hard chronological anchors
- 27 June 1349: Welch, Naya, original Klein die through Antigonus family notebook related event (volume wiki). Some general timeline summaries use 28 June for that same incident. Project convention is 27 June, mark discrepancy and don't build a puzzle from the calendar-date disagreement.
- 28 June 1349: Zhou Mingrui awakens as Klein in Tingen. First Tarot Club meeting with Audrey and Alger already occurs this day.
- 29 June: Klein involved with Blackthorn/Nighthawks and Old Neil.
- 2 July: Klein Sequence 9 Seer; second Tarot gathering.
- 29 August: Old Neil dies from loss of control.
- 31 August: Klein Sequence 8 Clown.
- September: Tingen tragedy, Klein's second death, resurrection and departure.

## AU deviations marked explicitly
- *D1*: A second cocoon releases its occupant same day as Klein; this did **not** happen in book-one canon.
- *D2*: A faint residual Sefirah Castle/gray-fog mark on second traveler partly reduces passive Hidden Sage knowledge attacks. Not proven for all transmigrators by canon; not equal to Klein's ownership/control. It can sometimes attract attention.
- *D3*: An unrelated deceased local copyist receives that traveler's consciousness. New life, address, printshop and associates are invented.
- *D4*: A locally published cautious police notice of Welch and Naya case, without mentioning the Antigonus notebook, appears shortly after incident. This is a narrative construction, not a verified canon newspaper article.
- *D5*: Main character can remember plot book one but memories are imperfect: translation name variants, dates, order, plot blind spots. Every intervention updates reliability of downstream predictions.
- *D6*: First original arc concerns altered catalog cards at Tingen print bindery. This is not part of canonical Antigonus notebook plot.

## Canon risks and automatic escalation
- Never have MC physically enter Sefirah Castle or access Tarot meeting just because gray fog mark exists.
- Never assume MC knew Klein has Sefirah Castle before identifying the setting; previous-life reader knows as abstract fiction, not proven reality.
- No Tarot insider leaks about gathering without a causal source.
- No early ad hoc friendship with Klein, Old Neil, Dunn, Azik or Leonard merely to accelerate plot.
- Church and occult organizations are powerful and suspicious; avoid convenient indiscriminate reveals of future plot or divine names.
- A Sequence 9 cannot outfight high sequence opponents. Use evidence, allies, costs, escape and strategic concealment.
- If an anchor changes, add `canonical_event`, `new_event`, `cause`, `direct_effect`, `ripple_prediction`, `review_due_chapter` to `continuity/canon-log.md`.
