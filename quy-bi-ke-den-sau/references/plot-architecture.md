# Long-form architecture: how 1,000 chapters can remain causal

## Opening: discovery staged, never instantaneous
1. Day 1 (28 Jun): familiar modern mind in unfamiliar industrial Victorian body, shock, body memories, dialect/language, livelihood, health, money. No names that instantly reveal source fiction.
2. Days 2–3: signs, geography, calendars, religious customs; learn the city's name is Tingen. Dismiss as coincidence until external corroboration.
3. Days 3–5: read locally distributed notice that Welch McGovern and Naya were linked to police inquiries. This printed notice is an **AU addition**; it must NOT publicly reveal Antigonus notebook, classified facts or Klein's supernatural status.
4. Days 5–9: confirm order and date; Klein is alive and joined Church investigation. Decide contact might trigger divination, interference and butterfly effects. Stay away for now.
5. Days 9–18: independently seek occult knowledge through mundane sources (trade, archives, printshop); first antagonists are noncanonical and have reasons of their own.
6. Chapters 18–30: Sequence 9 Mystery Pryer potion, first sensory/knowledge assault (residual gray fog only partly buffers), case resolved via evidence and teamwork, breadcrumbs point beyond Tingen.

## When Tarot Club continues without protagonist
- 28 June first meeting occurs without MC, later Monday gatherings continue.
- Treat Tarot Club as **off-screen independent causal engine**, not a session the protagonist somehow narrates from inside. Write a hidden Klein timeline note for author, but MC can only infer changes from indirect outcomes.
- Reader dramatic irony: know that Klein was doing something while OC worked elsewhere, but never reproduce entire original chapters.
- Possible future points of contact: shared black-market deal; library/auction item; a consequence of an investigation; coded anonymous help; rumor of the Fool organization; one crisis where both must act from separate locations.
- Long-term AU decision: MC DOES formally join Tarot Club as The Hermit in the original Cattleya entry window, after a causal change to Cattleya's situation. Cattleya remains autonomous and influential. See `tarot-and-world.md`.
- Klein has autonomy, makes his own breakthroughs; not an accessory to OC.

## Partnership and Klein's private investigation
- After MC joins, rotate knowledge-for-resource trades, shared cases with The World, and autonomous adventures. Partnership grows slowly to earned friendship.
- Klein independently investigates the extra gray-fog anomaly through informants, background checks, old-world cultural clues, and (much later) Sefirah Castle cocoon evidence. MC NEVER directs, investigates, or confesses to solve this. See `tarot-and-world.md`.

## Protagonist's own long objective
- SURVIVE: status as foreign soul and occult risk.
- FREE: gradually establish own potion formula and materials network independent of Church/Tarot, without becoming fanatical servant of Hidden Sage.
- DISCOVER: why a second cocoon opened, why a benign-looking catalog contains sealed information, who noticed the gray fog anomaly.
- CHOOSE: remain watcher, help Klein at key turns, or attempt Sequence 0 Hermit. Preserve an actual painful choice, rather than forcing apotheosis as guaranteed ending.

## Multi-view continuity
- `author_truth`: sealed answer, never reveal during blind solving.
- `MC_remembers_original`: prior-life novel knowledge, not first-hand fact; differentiate certain vs uncertain and date confidence.
- `MC_verified`: personally observed or reliable source.
- `Klein_offscreen`: author-facing canon anchor, not accessed by OC automatically.
- `reader_seen`: what has been printed in this fanfiction so far.
- After changed event, tag all future novel-memory references with `stable`, `at_risk`, or `unknown`; cannot use 'stable' without checking causal link.
