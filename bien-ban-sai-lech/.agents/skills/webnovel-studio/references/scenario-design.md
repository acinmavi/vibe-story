# Scenario Design — Fair-play mysteries

Use when creating or repairing a phó bản / case / anomalous-location mystery. This design combines tense rule-governed suspense, human conflict, investigation, and unexpected practical reasoning. Never reproduce an existing story's exact trials, NPCs, items or resolution.

## Deliverables in sequence

1. **Sealed truth** (author only): timeline of actual events; anomaly mechanism; source; activation and limits; adversary motive; cost; failure state; legitimate solutions; counterexamples.
2. **Rule interaction matrix**: each rule has a public-facing observation, a hidden truth, a cost and interactions with ≥2 other rules. It must create choices, not just atmosphere.
3. **Hypotheses**: at least 3 competing explanations, each supported by initially plausible evidence. Do not use fake impossibilities without justification.
4. **Clue ledger**: ID, chapter of first appearance, what is observed, interpretation initially expected, real interpretation, last chapter required for fair solution, whether verified. Everything necessary for the solution must appear **before** the reveal.
5. **Choice tree**: list ≥3 sensible courses of action with different risks; show why a reasonable expert might prefer each. Avoid making every companion foolish.
6. **Revelation schedule**: early signal, mid-point reframe, failed test, decisive differentiating clue, solution and cost, forward hook.
7. **Blind test**: give an independent solver only the evidence reader has seen at a chapter. Ask for hypotheses and predictions *with evidence*. Do not show the solver the sealed file, hidden title or later outlines.
8. **Counterfactual**: ask if the scenario still plays out coherently when hero chooses a different plausible strategy. Revise any point depending only on author's convenience.

## Red lines

- Never let heroes solve by unexplained ability, new rule, late secret witness or arbitrary luck.
- If a clue relies on specialized science, verify it or reduce the confidence of the character's inference. Never turn uncertain medicine, legal procedure or engineering into unquestionable fact.
- Bound the anomaly: activation conditions, scope, time, resource cost, loopholes and failure consequences. Avoid rule-of-the-week patches after reveal.
- Distinguish observation, hypothesis, deduction and proof in outline notes.
- Reveal should reinterpret ≥2 earlier clues, and the decisive clue must be findable before reveal.

## Acceptance checklist

- Each rule has at least one consequential decision.
- The solution is consistent with all publicly established observations.
- An informed reader can retrospectively reconstruct the causal chain.
- Character choices move events forward and have costs.
- At least one wrong but sensible hypothesis is explicitly tested and rejected.
