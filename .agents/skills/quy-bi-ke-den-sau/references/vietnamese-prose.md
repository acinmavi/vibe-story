# Vietnamese prose specification

Write clean serialized Vietnamese (not machine-translated Chinese). Third-person limited POV anchored to new traveler; occasional short interlude from other original characters AFTER arc 1, but not Klein's inaccessible inner mind unless explicitly approved. Favor concrete senses, physical effort, credible economic details of early industrial Tingen and layered dialogue. A chapter's engine is action and a difficult choice, not a five-paragraph lore lecture. Mix tension, dry humor and ordinary needs. Target 1,800–3,000 Vietnamese whitespace-delimited words when writing the final chapter draft. Avoid excessive Hán-Việt catchphrases, power-level exposition, memetic repetitive jokes. No copied passages or chapter paraphrases from source. End chapters on interesting decision, unresolved implication or consequence, not arbitrary noise.
