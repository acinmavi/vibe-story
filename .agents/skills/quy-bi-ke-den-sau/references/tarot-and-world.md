# Tarot, The World and the private investigation — non-negotiable AU arc design

## Canon boundary
- Klein = The Fool and his persona The World. No other person may act as separate owner of The World identity.
- In book-one canon, Cattleya is saved by Klein from the Hidden Sage's influence and receives The Hermit at chapter 564; her first full meeting is chapter 566. The AU reroutes the contingency, NOT Cattleya's right to exist, competence, status, ties to Bernadette, or relevance to the mystery.
- In book-one canon the cocoons are revealed gradually as Klein gains stronger control of Sefirah Castle (not to low-sequence Klein); the extra opened cocoon is the main explicit AU deviation. Any proof that someone shared the cocoon origin must remain late and earned.

## Why our protagonist is chosen instead of Cattleya
1. Early-to-mid arcs: the protagonist (MC) builds a small, credible but distinct archive and information source; he also meets a Cattleya-linked research broker. He never asserts that he is a privileged friend of Cattleya at first sight.
2. A chain of SMALL contingent interventions diverts Cattleya from the exact original accident with the Hidden Sage. For example: a warning about a catalog cipher causes the Cattleya-linked network to quarantine the object and alter travel plans. This is knowledge-based risk avoidance, not MC overpowering the Sage.
3. Klein separately observes an unusual gray-fog relationship surrounding MC while investigating a transaction or answering a risky prayer. This is a WEAK ANOMALY, not immediate proof of transmigration, and not proof of harmlessness.
4. Around Jan 1350 (the original join window; date conditional on the AU timeline), Klein weighs a low-to-middle Sequence scholar with unusual information value against established networks. He makes an active choice, asks terms, and recruits MC with the Hermit card. MC willingly chooses the name; never demand that a pathway obliges a Tarot card.
5. Cattleya stays Admiral of Stars, retains loyalty/complex tie to Bernadette, can advise/compete/negotiate with MC, and remains an autonomous major character. Trade routes original Cattleya would have provided to the Tarot Club MUST be accounted for: MC can broker equivalent suppliers but cannot have direct access to all of hers.

## Trade dynamics: information is powerful but costly

**Card etiquette is a HARD GATE**: read `references/tarot-etiquette.md`; OC may know source text details but must not monopolize a Tarot meeting. The Fool maintains authority, The World remains proactive, and members retain their own agency. Anomalous restraint may make Klein privately suspicious, never enough proof alone.

- MC's book-one plot memory is a FINITE and unstable starting resource, not an unlimited 'encyclopedia of any mystery'. Track every high-value exchange in `continuity/trade-ledger.json` with `information_origin`, `revealed_to`, `price_or_favor`, `exposure_risk`, `confidence`, `canon_divergence_risk`, `long_term_consequence`.
- Accept information-for-resources exchanges: potion formulas, leads, materials, protective artifacts, cash, favors, access to research, archival permission.
- The higher the information's mystical danger, the MORE important it is to restrict and contextualize. Never casually say names or reveal forbidden angel/deity secrets to low Sequence listeners. Klein remains discerning and may refuse a trade.
- Never make MC an endless Roselle diary replacement or all-knowing substitute for Klein's discovery; allow blind spots, wrong remembered translations, and future divergence.
- Some bargains fail. Some promises cost loyalty or leverage. MC must also earn information by normal research, collaborations, and original quests.

## Klein–MC partnership: graded trust, not instant brotherhood
I. **Transactional**: The World buys reliable minor intelligence, verifies it independently. MC learns The World is extremely capable, never publicly unmasks him. MC remembers his identity from the book but acts on no direct proof until corroborated; concealment costs social ease.
II. **Parallel action**: Two teams investigate linked issues from different sides. MC handles document forensics, sealed knowledge, preparations; The World handles field contacts, divination and confrontations. Disagreement creates tension; neither is an assistant to the other.
III. **Shared missions**: Joint investigations in changing locales; each saves the other ONCE in a credible, distinct way, pays a nontrivial price, accumulates non-mission conversation and shared memories.
IV. **Private trust**: After multiple sacrifices, The World volunteers a personal concern (not automatically his Fool identity); MC admits a vulnerability (not automatically transmigrator origin). They can work separately, still exchange help as friends.
V. **Peer confidants**: ONLY after Klein independently confirms cocoon connection, he chooses whether and when to tell MC what he found. MC does not steer, collaborate in, or preempt that investigation. Avoid an obligatory full reveal if fear and strategic danger still warrant restraint.

## Klein's independent investigation: separate author-facing subplot
**Prime constraint: MC NEVER helps investigate his own origin, never conveniently leaks a confession, never sends Klein a proof packet, never intentionally plants hints.** Natural unintentional traces generated by MC's separate life are allowed. For each lead, write who observed it, what was actually observed, what hypothesis it supports, what plausible alternative it has, and how Klein verifies it by his own initiative.

- Stage K0 (Tarot joining): weak gray-fog response during contact. Klein's working hypotheses: contact with sealed item, corrupted ritual, power trace, or divine favor; NOT 'he is a cocoon traveler'.
- Stage K1 (after separate missions): World, via his own information channels and payment, commissions a background check on MC's alias and Tingen whereabouts. Finds sudden behavioral rupture near 28 June 1349 and unusual script errors; still explainable by memory loss, possession, spiritual contamination.
- Stage K2 (middle arcs): World privately collects unrelated practical cultural evidence from MC's actions and archival records. Cross-check with Roselle's records and things Klein himself remembers from former life. Each isolated hint is weak; combined evidence says an OUTSIDER of a former era, not necessarily from a cocoon.
- Stage K3 (high Sequence, after access to cocoon site becomes plausible): Klein examines Sefirah Castle's door/cocoons in his own exploration and finds an additional opened site or residual fourth release trace (AU). He links its chronology and spiritual fingerprint with the pattern seen in MC. If canon sequence/timing cannot justify access yet, DEFER PROOF.
- Stage K4 (late, after independent cross-confirmation): Klein has enough evidence to privately conclude shared origin. He decides how to approach MC; the scene should reward accumulated relationship rather than make MC pass a test. Protect crucial original canon reveals in Klein's own emotional arc.

`continuity/klein-investigation.json` is author-only. Mark it `author_only` and do NOT insert any evidence into MC viewpoint until MC independently could know it. The future result must not be conveniently asserted without the verified observations written in chapter references.

## Controlled participation in book-one anchors
- Ch 1–30: no Tarot, no direct interaction. Independent survival, Tingen and Mystery Pryer path.
- Before recruitment: maintain unrelated small cases and a Cattleya-linked network while The World rises according to canon. At most occasional passive news/supplied information; don't mirror canonical scenes.
- Recruitment window: fork one specific cause of Cattleya's canonical meeting, explain Cattleya's new whereabouts and risks, invite MC for his independent value to the club; never make Mr. Fool act out of character.
- After recruitment: a combination of **one Tarot trade arc, one private case, one World cooperation arc**; rotate order and ratio. Do not have MC accompany every Klein adventure. Preserve canonical outcomes where feasible, but if MC causes divergence, record concrete downstream impact rather than silently resetting canon.
- Late: joint mysteries grow consequential; some secrets belong to Klein alone, some to MC alone. Keep readers unsure when they will compare notes.

## Quality gates
A collaboration arc fails if (a) MC merely gives Klein the canon solution, (b) Klein is reduced to a fighter summoned on demand, (c) MC receives Sequence advancement free for a spoiler, (d) the relationship develops only through favors and no real personal stakes, (e) Klein learns cocoon origin before relevant Sefirah Castle access, or (f) Cattleya is erased without causal continuity.
