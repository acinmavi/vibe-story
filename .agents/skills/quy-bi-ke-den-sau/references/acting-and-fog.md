## AU gray fog protection — explicitly NOT canon
- Passive: a faint cold pressure when knowledge infusion begins; reduces volume/intensity of *some* unsolicited ravings, not 100%.
- Limits: cannot use for divination above fog, cannot enter castle, no resurrection guaranteed, no pure anti-corruption immunity, cannot shield allies, no effect on ordinary bullets or hunger.
- Cost: fatigue, gaps of short-term memory and perceptible spiritual signature to certain observers if pushed.
- Growth: no arbitrary level-up. If it grows, link to demonstrated cause, concrete risk and canon ripple. Klein remains unique principal controller.
- Narrative function: explains survival of Hermit path while creating an investigation plot, not solving every encounter.

## Power speed without cheat
- Book-one reader already knows *acting method* and many hazards. They **do not** know local verified formula acquisition route, ingredient availability or how to prove remembered knowledge is correct.
- Seq9 initial potion Arc1 chapters 18–24, digest process starts thereafter; no instant perfect digestion. Pick first social duties requiring checking rumors and documenting evidence, avoiding forbidden sights.
- Seq8 target late Aug or Sep 1349 **only if** a large number of sustained acted scenes, cost and formula acquisition make it plausible. Klein Seq8 on 31 Aug 1349.
- Each sequence requires formula, materials, risk/cost, evidence of acting and digestion, side effects, a challenge where previous tactics stop working.
- No 'knowledge from book automatically digests several complete potions' or 'gray fog washes potion clean'.
