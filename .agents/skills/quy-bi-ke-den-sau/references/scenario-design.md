# Mystery-first design (for this fanfiction)

Start with the supernatural mechanism and its physical limits; choose culprit and goal; build the real timeline; derive observable traces; devise 2 false hypotheses that fit some clues; require one independent test; calculate consequences for canon timeline and MC resources; then distribute clues BEFORE correct reveal. No secret rule announced at climax.

Arc 1: `arcs/arc-001/solution-sealed.md` is author-only. The original 'catalog card rewriting' is deliberately local: an anomalous sealed object changes one *catalog record/registered tag* on a limited schedule, not cosmic memory or every document. Validate range, triggers, old records, restoration and cost so low-sequence actors can eventually solve it.

Conflict should not require making Klein or Nighthawks foolish. Their investigations are focused on a separate major incident. Redirection could expose OC to church investigation; do not make them ignore credible severe threats without reason.

For each chapter: mystery gain, cost, active choice, tension variation, piece of new evidence. Arc climax must involve two previously shown rules and a personal sacrifice.
